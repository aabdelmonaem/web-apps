Killer Sudoku Web Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Ahmed Elbari, an individual developer based in Qatar, provides Killer Sudoku. In this policy, “I”, “me”, and “the developer” mean Ahmed Elbari. This policy describes the current browser version of Killer Sudoku and the shared website theme preference. For privacy questions or requests, email a.abdelmonaem@gmail.com, stating the game name.

2. Overview

The current web game is free, requires no account, and contains no advertising or purchases. Killer Sudoku puzzle generation, cage validation, and gameplay run in your browser. The current application code does not include analytics, advertising trackers, automatic crash reporting, or a service that uploads puzzle progress.

3. Gameplay and browser storage

The puzzle, solution, cage layout and totals, entered numbers, pencil notes, selected cell, difficulty, score, mistakes, hints, pause state, and timer are held in browser memory while the game page is open. Progress is not saved to browser storage or a developer-operated server. Reloading or navigating away discards the current game.

The shared website stores your selected light or dark appearance in localStorage under the key puzzle-theme. It reads that preference when you return. If no preference is saved, the game checks your browser’s preferred color scheme. The theme still works if storage is unavailable.

4. Cookies and device permissions

The current application code does not set cookies or request your location, camera, microphone, Bluetooth, contacts, or local-network device discovery. It does not read your device name or create a player profile. The theme preference uses localStorage rather than a cookie.

5. Website delivery and hosting

Your browser requests the website’s HTML, scripts, styles, and images from the server hosting it. Serving a website necessarily exposes connection information, such as an IP address and requested resource, to that server. Hosting or network providers may process request logs under their own practices. The application code does not establish what a particular deployment logs or how long those logs are retained.

6. External links

Legal and support pages may contain links to external websites or an email address. Following an external link is subject to that provider’s privacy practices. An email link opens your configured email application; it does not automatically send a message.

7. Support email

If you send a support email, the developer receives your email address, message, and any name, screenshots, or other information you include. These are used to respond, investigate issues, handle privacy requests, and meet applicable legal obligations. Avoid sending passwords or unnecessary personal information.

The support mailbox uses Gmail. Google and your email provider process messages under their own terms and privacy practices. See Google’s Privacy Policy. Messages may be processed outside Qatar, including in countries where those providers operate. The developer does not sell support information, use it for targeted advertising, or send marketing emails. Disclosure may occur where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or valid legal process.

8. Retention and deletion

Restart or New game replaces the current in-memory Killer Sudoku gameplay state. Reloading or leaving the game page discards that progress. To remove the saved theme preference, clear this website’s site data or local storage using your browser settings. This may also remove other preferences stored by this website; it does not delete emails or hosting logs.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond to and document requests, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion by email. Information legally required to be retained may be kept for the necessary period; the developer will explain any applicable restriction. Email-provider backups and hosting records are subject to their providers’ retention practices.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

You can change the theme, clear the website’s browser storage, or stop using the site. For personal information held by the developer, you may request access, correction, deletion, or other rights available under applicable law by contacting a.abdelmonaem@gmail.com. Only information reasonably needed to verify and handle the request will be requested. Where processing relies on consent, withdrawing it does not undo earlier lawful processing.

You may also complain to the relevant privacy authority. In Qatar, information is available from the National Cyber Security Agency’s Personal Data Privacy Protection Department. Rights depend on applicable law; browser-local records must generally be managed on your device.

10. General audience and children

Killer Sudoku is a general-audience puzzle game. The current web game does not ask for a player’s age, real name, or account. Parents and guardians should supervise support communications. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer without necessary permission, contact the support address so it can be investigated and deleted where required.

11. Security and policy changes

No browser, network, or storage method is guaranteed secure. Protect your device and avoid sharing sensitive information in support attachments. This policy will be updated when the web game’s behavior or applicable requirements change. Revisions will show an updated date; any additional notice or consent required for materially different processing will be provided.
