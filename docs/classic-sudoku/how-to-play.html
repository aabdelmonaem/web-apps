How to Play

A quick guide to Classic Sudoku in your browser.

1. Choose a difficulty

Click New game, then choose Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert, Master, or Extreme. Choosing a level replaces the current puzzle and resets your progress. Close the picker to keep your current game.

2. Check the row, column, and box

Fill the board with digits 1 to 9. Every row, column, and 3 × 3 box must contain each digit exactly once. Original clues are fixed.

3. Select a cell and enter a number

Click or tap a cell, then choose a number on the number pad. Your selection highlights its row, column, and box; matching digits are highlighted too. Use Erase to clear an editable cell. The number pad disables a digit once it appears nine times, unless Notes is on.

4. Add pencil notes

Turn Notes on and choose an empty cell. Tap digits to add small pencil marks; tap a marked digit again to remove it. Turn Notes off to enter a final answer. Filled cells cannot receive notes. Entering a final number clears that cell’s notes and removes matching notes from its row, column, and box.

5. Watch your lives

Each changed incorrect final entry removes one heart. The third mistake ends the game. Entering the same value again does not add another mistake. Red digits show a duplicate in a row, column, or box; an answer can still be incorrect without a visible duplicate.

6. Use a hint

Each game has three hints. Hints immediately fills the selected empty or incorrect editable cell with its answer. If the selection is a clue or is already correct, it fills the first empty or incorrect cell instead. Each reveal uses one hint and earns no points.

7. Pause or restart

Pause hides the board, stops the timer, and blocks number entry and hints. Resume game continues play. Restart replays the same puzzle with all entries and notes cleared, three lives and three hints restored, score and timer reset, and Notes off.

8. Finish the puzzle

Fill every cell with its correct answer to win. The timer stops after a win or the third mistake, and further number entry and hints are blocked. Use Restart or New game to play again.

Keyboard controls

Select or focus a board cell first. Use 1–9 to enter numbers, arrow keys to move the selection, Backspace, Delete, or 0 to erase, and N to toggle Notes.

Your current game

Progress lasts while this game page remains open. Reloading the page or navigating away starts over when you return. To read a guide without leaving your puzzle, open its link in a new browser tab.
